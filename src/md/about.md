## About me

Hi! I’m Javier, a product manager at Mecalux Software Solutions and Software Engineering graduate from the University of Oviedo. I believe development should be value-driven, quick to iterate, clearly communicated and well documented. I use AI used wherever it genuinely helps the team move faster.

Coming from hands-on full-stack development, with a growing interest in cybersecurity, I moved into product through requirements engineering. My final degree project, IR-Board, a requirements management platform, is where both sides met: turning what stakeholders need into something a team can build, trace and maintain.

After all, software should be predictable and frictionless for clients, and sustainable for developers.
